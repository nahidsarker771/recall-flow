What's inside

Built around the forgetting curve, not around engagement metrics.

Everything is local. No login, no sync, no “premium” wall between you and your own notes.

A scheduler with taste

Guided mode walks new topics up 1 → 7 → 16 → 35 days, then grows with your ease factor. Classic SM-2 is there if you want the textbook version.

90-minute focus sessions

A real foreground timer that survives screen-off and app switching, with milestone nudges at 1, 5, 10, 15, 30, 45, 60, 75 and 90 minutes.

A two-day calendar

Month grid plus today and tomorrow side by side. Tap any empty hour to add a review at exactly that time.

Stats without the guilt

Retention curve, weekly bars, rating distribution and a mastery score. Honest numbers, no streak-shaming.

Reminders that respect you

One nudge per topic per day, at your chosen reminder time. Exact alarms when Android allows, graceful fallback when it doesn't.

Your data, exportable

Full JSON export and import through Android's file picker. The database is yours; take it anywhere.

About the project

A study tracker that respects your attention.

RecallFlow is a native Android app written in Kotlin + Jetpack Compose, with Room for storage and a single shared ViewModel. No web wrapper, no tracking SDK, no network layer at all.

The app asks for notifications and exact-alarm access — and not the internet permission. Your topics, review history and settings never leave the device.

“The best study app is the one you close after ten minutes because you actually did the review.”

Rating a review 0–5 updates the ease factor and picks the next interval. Rating below 3 resets the topic to one day. Mastery combines interval and repetition count, capped at 100.
